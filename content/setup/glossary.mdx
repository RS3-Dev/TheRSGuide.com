## Defensive Abilities, Tanking & Positioning

<TableScroll>
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Term</th>
        <th>Description</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <td>Aggro</td>
        <td>Aggression or NPC aggression</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>Base</td>
        <td>Base tank or main tank</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>Cade</td>
        <td>Barricade, a defensive ability that reduces most damage taken to 0</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>Cept</td>
        <td>Intercept, a spell that redirects incoming damage from another player to you</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>DD</td>
        <td>Death dot; a command to stand on the same tile as other players</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>Devo</td>
        <td>Devotion, a defensive ability that makes protection prayers 100% effective</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>Immort</td>
        <td>Immortality, a defensive ability that revives you if you die while it is active</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>MD</td>
        <td>Melee distance; standing within melee range of your target</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>Res/Reso</td>
        <td>Resonance, a defensive ability that can heal you instead of taking damage from an attack</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>Voke</td>
        <td>Provoke, a defensive ability that draws your target's aggression away from a teammate</td>
      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</TableScroll>
