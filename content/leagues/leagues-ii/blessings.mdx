## Overview
Blessings are powerful buffs which enhance your combat abilities in the league. Complete Blessing Tasks to unlock each tier of blessing.

Each tier is divided into three paths: Chaos, Balance and Order. Tiers 4 and 8 are God Tier blessings dictated by your choices from the previous three tiers. If one of each path is chosen, the Balance God Tier Blessing is unlocked. Otherwise whichever path the majority of blessings belong to will be unlocked.
