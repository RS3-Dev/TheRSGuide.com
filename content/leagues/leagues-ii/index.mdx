## League Overview

The Equilibrium League, also known as Leagues II: Equilibrium, is RuneScape's second League. It starts on August 10th 2026 and runs through September 21. This time, the League is themed around Order, Balance, and Chaos, the belief systems of Saradomin, Guthix, and Zamorak. Like Catalyst League, Leagues II will be region-locked. In addition to region locking, Leagues II will also feature dedicated free-to-play worlds for the first three tiers of the league. Every player will be an Ironman, so you will have the usual restrictions, including being unable to trade with other players.

## Progression and Unlocks

### Relic Tiers

You will unlock relic tiers as you earn points. Tier 1 starts at 10 points, followed by Tier 2 at 750, Tier 3 at 1,750, Tier 4 at 3,500, Tier 5 at 6,000, Tier 6 at 12,000, and Tier 7 at 20,000. The point requirements from Tier 2 to Tier 3 and from Tier 5 to Tier 7 are lower this year. Tier 7 requires a couple thousand fewer points than it did last year.

### Blessing Tiers

Blessing tiers are unlocked by completing Blessing tasks. You will need 1 task for Tier 1, 3 tasks for Tier 2, 5 tasks for Tier 3, 9 tasks for Tier 4, 12 tasks for Tier 5, 16 tasks for Tier 6, 20 tasks for Tier 7, and 26 tasks for Tier 8. Tier 4 is God Tier 1, while Tier 8 is God Tier 2.

### Region Unlocks

You will begin in Misthalin and Havenhythe. Karamja is your third region and unlocks during the tutorial once you have completed 50 tasks. Your remaining regions unlock at 175 tasks for Region 4, 300 tasks for Region 5, and 450 tasks for Region 6.
