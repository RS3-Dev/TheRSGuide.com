- The Equilibrium League, also known as Leagues II: Equilibrium, is the second iteration of the Leagues game mode in RuneScape.
- It starts on 10 August 2026 and finishes on 21 September 2026.
- It is themed around Order, Balance, and Chaos: the belief systems of Saradomin, Guthix, and Zamorak.
- Unlike Catalyst League, it will have dedicated free-to-play worlds.
- As with all Leagues, all players are Ironmen and have the same restrictions, including being unable to trade with other players.
- Tier thresholds:
  - Tier 1: 10 points
  - Tier 2: 750 points
  - Tier 3: 1,750 points
  - Tier 4: 3,500 points
  - Tier 5: 6,000 points
  - Tier 6: 12,000 points
  - Tier 7: 20,000 points
- The points required from Tier 2 to Tier 3 and from Tier 5 to Tier 7 have been reduced this year.
- Tier 7 requires a couple thousand fewer points than last year.
- Blessing task thresholds:
  - Tier 1: 1 Blessing task
  - Tier 2: 3 Blessing tasks
  - Tier 3: 5 Blessing tasks
  - Tier 4 (God Tier 1): 9 Blessing tasks
  - Tier 5: 12 Blessing tasks
  - Tier 6: 16 Blessing tasks
  - Tier 7: 20 Blessing tasks
  - Tier 8 (God Tier 2): 26 Blessing tasks
- Region unlocks:
  - Starter regions: Misthalin and Havenhythe
  - Region 3: Karamja, unlocked during the tutorial at 50 tasks
  - Region 4: 175 tasks
  - Region 5: 300 tasks
  - Region 6: 450 tasks
