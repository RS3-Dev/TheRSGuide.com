## The RS Guy's build path

This is The RS Guy's planned build path for Leagues II, including the regions, relics, and blessings Ryan expects to use as he progresses.

![The RS Guy's planned Leagues II build path](https://media.thersguide.com/leagues-2/media/the-rs-guy-s-build.webp)

The blessing picks shown in the image above are only the starting point for Ryan's build. Once he has used Power Archive to stockpile enough Invention components, he plans to spend one of his blessing resets and move into the final setup shown below.

### Initial blessing path

The opening path uses **Tempered Heart** at Tier 7 to unlock **Power Archive** at Tier 8. Power Archive doubles the rank of eligible combat perks stored inside it, giving you access to a much higher rank of Scavenging. While you AFK your combat skills, that boosted Scavenging perk will build a large stockpile of Invention components along the way. This also means you can skip the **Perkfection** relic entirely, since Power Archive only needs to stay active long enough to gather the components you need.

### Blessing path after the reset

Once you have enough components saved, use a blessing reset to swap **Tempered Heart** for **Perfidious**. This also changes the Tier 8 God-tier blessing from **Power Archive** to **Chaotic Insight**, completing the final combat-focused path. Power Archive and the boosted Scavenging perk have already done their job by this point, and the components they gathered will carry you through the rest of the league.

## Alternative builds

### Less Experienced

This build is for players who lean on basic attacks more than empowered abilities. Necromancy is a natural fit, and the setup does not expect you to manage a complicated ability rotation.

Take note of **Higher Power** at Tier 5 here. This blessing removes your access to Berserk, Death's Swiftness, Living Death, and Sunshine. If you do not plan to pick Kandarin, that downside matters much less because Kandarin is required to unlock those abilities. However, we do not recommend this build if you plan to pick Kandarin specifically for those unlocks. Higher Power will eventually take the abilities away, which can turn Kandarin into a wasted region choice.

### Saradomin Sword Swinger/Gloomfire Goblin

Take this path if you plan to spam the Saradomin Sword or Gloomfire Bow special attack and watch the damage pile up. It starts with Chaos through Tier 4, then uses Order picks later in the build to reach an Order God-tier blessing.

This build really comes online at Tier 8, when **Genesis Essence** treats your equipped weapons as Tier 120. That is a huge jump for the Tier 70 Saradomin Sword and another sizable upgrade for the Tier 90 Gloomfire Bow. Their special attacks also gain damage from the higher weapon tier, so Genesis Essence adds even more damage on top of what makes those special attacks strong in the first place. Since most of the payoff arrives at Tier 8, this may be a better build to respec into after reaching Tier 8 with a different path.

### Magic and Ranged Critical Strike

This one is built around reaching 50% critical strike chance with Magic or Ranged. Once you hit the cap, Unholy Critual turns the extra critical strike chance into more critical strike damage. Inferno of Zamorak can also critically strike when paired with Unholy Critual, which lets it recursively trigger itself. If one of your attacks critically strikes and releases an Inferno of Zamorak, that Inferno can critically strike and release another Inferno while you continue dealing damage, which can lead to insane amounts of damage output if you get lucky.
