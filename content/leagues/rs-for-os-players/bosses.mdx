## Overview
If you’re coming from OSRS you may have expectations that bosses that exist in both games are at a similar point in your player progression. While this is accurate on occasion, it is generally not the case. As an example, while General Graardor is a relatively punishing boss in OSRS, it’s one of the first bosses you’ll be able to take on in RuneScape. If you’re looking for basic guides, similar to OSRS you’ll see good coverage on YouTube, but for more specific, advanced methods we recommend referencing the [PVME](https://pvme.io), or PvM Encyclopedia.
