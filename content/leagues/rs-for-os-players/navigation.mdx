## Lodestones

The bread and butter of navigation in RS3. Free to use, with a cost of law runes for instant travel. Most need to be simply clicked on once to activate, while some need to be built. See the [Lodestone Network](/setup/map#lodestone-network) section for more information on unlocking and using them.

## Jewelry

Much like OSRS, we have a large quantity of teleportation jewelry. Some are identical, like the Amulet of Glory or Games Necklace. Some have one or two different teleports, like the Ring of Dueling or Ring of Respawn. Others are completely unique, like the Enlightened Amulet in RS3 compared to the Burning Amulet in OSRS. There are also items that can give this jewelry infinite charges with Invention.

## Spirit Trees & Fairy Rings

Nearly unchanged from OSRS. These also have Invention counterparts that allow you to use them from anywhere with an item in your inventory. Some trees and rings need to be grown or repaired.

## Spells

Much like OSRS, we have a large quantity of teleportation spells across all three spellbooks. Most of these are unchanged. You will also notice that some spells under the Teleport category are green. The amazing reason for this is that they cost no runes and can be used on any spellbook.

## Items

If you are familiar with teleport items such as Drakan's medallion in OSRS, fret not, we have these too. We also have teleport tabs that can be made at a POH. An additional function of teletabs in RS3 is the ability to "chip" them for an altered destination. For example, a normal Varrock teleport goes to Varrock Square or the Grand Exchange. A "chipped" Varrock teleport goes to the Varrock Museum. For most players these are pretty niche, but they can be a massive help for players looking to complete clues. These can be used and stored in an item known as Dave's spellbook after the Evil Dave's Big Day Out quest.

## POH

With the addition of 120 Construction, the Player Owned House has had a massive amount of teleport items and furniture added. It also has an amazing green teleport that lets you go to your POH at any time, on any spellbook, with no rune cost and a 50 Construction requirement. Some of these, like the Jewelry Box, Portal Nexus, Fairy Ring, and Spirit Tree, will be familiar to players coming from OSRS. Search the wiki for destinations and choose which furniture is best for your house.

## Run Energy

The same system from OSRS returns but is greatly improved. Our run energy drains at a much slower rate, weight impacts it less, and you are given two systems for easy recharging. The Rest system can be used by right-clicking the run orb on your minimap. You will sit your butt down and take a chill pill, recharging run energy at an accelerated rate. This cannot be used in combat. We also have musicians scattered throughout the world that you can listen to for an insanely fast run recharge. As an OSRS player myself, these systems would be a godsend, to be honest.

## Movement

Adding onto the run system, we have a few movement abilities. Surge sends your character 10 tiles forward in the direction you are facing. Escape does the inverse. There is also a codex to unlock a second charge of Surge and Escape. Barge and Greater Barge require melee weapons and will launch your character into melee distance of your target while dealing some damage. Dive is the most unique movement ability. Once you click it, you can click any tile to travel there. This ability has the unique quirk of allowing you to phase through some walls and terrain. In addition, Surge + Dive is a common combo for crossing up to 20 tiles at once.
