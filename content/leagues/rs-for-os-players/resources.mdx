In OSRS, you might be familiar with how smithing and crafting have relatively high requirements compared to the strength of the gear you make. For example, a rune platebody requires 99 Smithing to make. In RS3, our resources have a rough tiering system to them. Some resources follow a fairly specific curve, with 10 level increments in order to gather, process and sometimes equip them (Mining and Smithing). In other cases, it’s less of a scale and more similar to OSRS, but there are many resources with deviated, or differing requirements between the two games. This table is a visual guide and reference that will let you know which resources are unlocked, and may be used at each level bracket.
