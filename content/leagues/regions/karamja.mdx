The Karamja region is the second region that all players are given access to in Leagues II Equilibrium. While seemingly not having much to offer, there are a few important pieces of combat related content included here, such as the Brimhaven Dungeon as well as the TzHaar Fight Caves and Fight Kiln.

## Notable Gear

In the TzHaar City you will find the TzHaar Fight Caves and Fight Kiln. These are wave based gauntlet style encounters that test your combat capabilities and reward you with the iconic Fire Cape (Fight Caves), and you can sacrifice this Fire Cape for access to the Fight Caves where you can earn the TokHaar capes. These TokHaar capes are required to be able to craft the Igneous capes from the TzKal-Zuk encounter, and these are the best capes in the game for every combat style.
