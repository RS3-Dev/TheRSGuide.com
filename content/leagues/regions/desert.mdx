The Desert is a massive region in RuneScape with a huge variety of skilling methods offered as well as two major cities in Al Kharid and Menaphos. Players who choose the Desert will also gain access to Mazcab and by virtue the Liberation of Mazcab Raid and Goebie Supply Shop, as well as the Kalphite Queen and King, and the Heart of Gielinor; also known as God Wars Dungeon 2. The Desert is also home to the current newest endgame boss; Amascut, the Devourer, and all of the powerful rewards she holds. There is also the Vault of Hereditas Thieving Heist as well as the Sophanem Slayer Dungeon and access to the Ancient Magicks Spellbook is also found in the Desert. For Skilling opportunities there is also Het’s Oasis which has training methods for Agility, Farming, and Hunter.

## Notable Gear

Access to the Heart of Gielinor provides the player with T80 power armor and T85 weapons for Melee, Magic, and Ranged from bosses such as Vindicta & Gorvek, Helwyr, Gregorovic, and the Twin Furies; as well as T92 weapons, most notably the Seren Godbow, from Telos. The drops from the Heart of Gielinor can also be disassembled for powerful Invention components used for perks such as Aftershock and Caroming. Amascut offers T95 Magic power armor as well as a T95 Necromancy Guard and a T95 2H Melee weapon while also being another source for Shards of Genesis Essence; an item used to turn any T95 weapon into a T100 weapon. The Desert is also home to the Kalphite King, dropping an assortment of T90 Melee weapons as well as Perfect Chitin. The Magister is accessed through the Sophanem Slayer Dungeon and can award players with the Gloves of Passage which are the best Melee gloves in the game. The Liberation of Mazcab Raid can provide players with Tank armor for Melee, Magic, and Ranged as well as new ability unlocks and The Vault of Hereditas can drop T90 weapons for Magic and Ranged that have powerful special attacks, as well as a Necromancy upgrade.
