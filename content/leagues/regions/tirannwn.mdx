The elven realm of Tirannwn is home to Prifddinas, the largest city with many major skilling related resources such as Cleansing Crystal Prayer training, the Hefin Agility Course, Ithell Harps for Harmonic Dust which is required for Crystal weapons and tools, Waterfall Fishing, as well as the Rush of Blood activity. Prifddinas also has many high level Thieving targets across the city and Tirannwn also contains The Lost Grove which has many high level Slayer creatures and the Solak boss encounter.

## Notable Gear

Solak is the most notable source of upgrades here, being the sole source of Erethdor’s Grimoire, a powerful Crit related pocket slot item, and Blightbound Crossbows which are T92 Ranged dual wield weapons. Cinderbane Gloves are also dropped from Solak along with the Slayer creatures outside of the boss instance, and Dark Beasts can also be found in this region should you be looking to obtain a Dark Bow. Players can also craft Attuned Crystal equipment in Prifddinas which is T80 as well as augmentable Crystal Tools.
