Anachronia is the home of the dinosaurs in RuneScape, as well as being tied to much of the Dragonkin lore. Because of this, you’ll find content related to these things such as multiple high level Archaeology Dig Sites, the Rex Matriarchs and Raksha boss encounters as well as multiple different high level Slayer monsters. Also located here is the Anachronia Base Camp and the Herby Werby D&D, Big Game Hunter, the Dream of Iaia, ‘Accidental” Fletching/Firemaking, and the Asuran Arsenal; which are all very strong training methods for a variety of skills. With Anachronia also comes the Dinosaur Farm which is a higher level version of the Player Owned Farm where you can get many endgame related resources to make useful consumables such as Vulnerability Bombs.

## Notable Gear

The Rex Matriarchs are the successor to the Dagannoth Kings and just like their predecessors, drop combat related rings for each given combat style; however these upgraded Rings are the best rings for each combat style in the game. The Raksha boss encounter offers relevant ability upgrades for the Magic and Ranged combat styles, as well as the fan favorite Divert ability. In the Asuran Arsenal Thieving Heist you’ll be able to obtain Varanus’s Mercy and the Nodon Spike Harness which are powerful endgame Melee upgrades. Skeka’s Hypnowand, a major Hunter skilling offhand  is also found on Anachronia
