The Fremennik Province is one of the more sparse regions in RuneScape, not really offering much to the player in terms of access. The Dagannoth Kings, a source of rings for each combat style can be found here as well as access to a Slayer Dungeon, the Managing Miscellania activity which can provide a lot of useful early resources, and an Agility course. Access to the Lunar Spellbook can also be gained though the Lunar Island.

## Notable Drops

As said before, the only real notable drops here are the rings from the Dagannoth Kings; however they also drop a Dragon Hatchet, which is used to make higher tier augmentable hatchets. A Dragon Pickaxe can also be obtained here from the Chaos Dwarf Battlefield, similarly used in the process to make higher tier augmentable pickaxes.
