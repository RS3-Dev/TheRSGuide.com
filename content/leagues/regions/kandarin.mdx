Kandarin is home to multiple cities such as Yanille, Ardougne, and Seers’ Village while also providing access to the Player Owned Farm, Fishing Guild (and therefore Deep Sea Fishing), a few Agility Courses, Eternal Magic Trees, two Archaeology Dig Sites, a Slayer Dungeon. Kandarin also grants access to Burial Smithing similar to Asgarnia, and also is the region players must unlock should they want access to Ancient Invention. The Penguin D&D rewards are also gained here, although you might not be able to get the maximum points as they’re scattered across the entire world.

## Notable Gear

The Ascension Dungeon has minor bosses known as the Legiones, and combined with the Slayer monsters in the Dungeon can provide players Ascension Crossbows; T90 Dual Wield Ranged weapons. Dark Beasts can also be found in Kandarin which means players can obtain a Dark Bow, a weapon commonly used in an Essence of Finality in the end game for the Ranged combat style. You’ll also be able to craft T90 Magic and Ranged 2H weapons here through Woodcutting + Fletching/Runecrafting, albeit at a high level. The Sunshine and Death’s Swiftness abilities can also be unlocked here, along with a few notable Slayer drops such as Nightmare Gauntlets and the Dark Bow.
