Morytania, while being a relatively small region offers a few notable PvM encounters like Araxxor and The Barrows as well as the Slayer Tower which is a training spot for mid level Slayer, an Agility course, and an Archaeology Dig Site. The city of Canifis and Vyrelords, a highly popular combat training method which also drops many useful resources.

## Notable Gear

With Araxxor comes Noxious weaponry, T90 2H weapons for Melee, Magic, and Ranged and Noxious Components which are used to make higher tiers of the Biting Invention perk. The Barrows and The Rise of the Six encounters can also be found here offering assorted T70 armor and weapons for Melee, Magic, and Ranged; as well as the T90 shields from The Rise of the Six which are the best shields in the game. Another notable mention is the Salve Amulet is rewarded from a Quest here, giving an extremely powerful boost against Undead targets.
