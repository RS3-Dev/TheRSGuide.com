## Choosing Your Regions

RuneScape is divided up to 11 different regions, each home to different cities and content. During Leagues you will be locked to a maximum of 6 total regions, 3 of which are forced unlocks and 3 being your choice. This means that you’ll be able to pick and choose what sort of content you want to experience in your Leagues adventure. All players will share a few starting regions, but shortly after beginning your leagues journey, you’ll be able to start adding in the regions of your choice. While you’ll get access to almost everything in your chosen regions, you’ll also be locked out of the content outside your regions, so it’s important to have an idea what content exists in each region.

### Quests and Area Tasks

With each region will also come a list of autocompleted quests and things such as Treasure Trails will only give you steps that you are able to access. Not every quest will be autocompleted, but most larger unlocks will be, especially if the quest would usually require entry to multiple different regions that may or may not be available to you. You’ll have access to each region's Area Task sets as well, and all the non auto completed quests are fair game to do for yourself should you choose to do so. Autocompleted quests will grant their usual rewards, excluding any experience lamps.

## Make the League Your Own

It’s worth mentioning there is no right or wrong way to go about Leagues, and you should pick the content that aligns with what you want out of your Leagues journey. If you think you’ll enjoy Solak, then pick Tirannwn and go for it; if you want to experience Player Owned Farms, take Kandarin and have fun. RuneScape Leagues is designed to be shaped differently for each player, so take advantage of that and make it your own. At the same time, if you’re unsure what to do or where to start, we’ve got a [pathway guide](/leagues/leagues-ii/routes) that will power you through the first few regions, and the first 3.5 tiers of relics, as well as your first region choice.
