In RuneScape Leagues, every player starts off in the Misthalin region as well as having access to the Havenhythe region. You’re going to find a little bit of everything here, including but not limited to; multiple major cities, various Archaeology Dig Sites and the Archaeology Guild, Fort Forinthry, the Tears of Guthix D&D, access to a Runecrafting training option in the form of the Runespan as well as the Runecrafting Guild, and plenty of PvM content ranging from easy early game bosses all the way up to end game bosses that drop some of the best weapons RuneScape has to offer. Access to the Necromancy combat style is also available through The City of Um.

## Notable Gear

As stated before, many of the best weapons in RuneScape can be found here in the Elder God Wars Dungeon, such as; the Ek-ZekKil, Fractured Staff of Armadyl, Bow of the Last Guardian, and the Shards of Leng and you’ll also have access to the best capes in the game for each combat style from the TzKal-Zuk encounter. Along with Rasial access comes the full set of complete best-in-slot armor and weapons for Necromancy, and the Sanctum of Rebirth offers extremely powerful and easy to obtain Magic Dual Wield weapons, as well as the Shard of Genesis Essence; turning any T95 weapon into a T100 weapon. You’ll also be able to obtain skilling upgrades from the Gate of Elidinis and Croesus encounters, both of RuneScape’s skilling bosses. Players can also access the Sunshine and Death’s Swiftness Magic and Ranged abilities through Zamorak, as well as the Chaos Roar Melee ability. Other abilities that can be unlocked include Greater Concentrated Blast, Invoke Lord of Bones, Magma Tempest, and Prayer unlocks in Divine Rage and Eclipsed Soul.

It is worth noting that the common drops of many of the bosses in this region are extremely helpful to have access to and can aid you a lot on your journey giving alternative access to some resources provided you are capable of defeating them, so be sure to check the drop tables for anything that might be useful.
