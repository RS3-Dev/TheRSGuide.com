Asgarnia is a large region with a variety of content similar to Misthalin, ranging from early all the way to late game. Many cities such as Taverly, Burthorpe, Port Sarim, and Falador and access to skilling locations including the Artisan’s Workshop (a requirement for the highest xp/hr Smithing training methods) as well as Player-Owned Ports/The Arc can be found here. There’s also plenty of PvM related content to be found here as well including the God Wars Dungeon, the Queen Black Dragon, and the Temple of Aminishi which can provide many mid and late game PvM upgrades.

## Notable Gear

The God Wars Dungeon offers a deeply extensive list of T70 items from 4 different bosses as well as T80 gear from Nex for each of the 3 original combat styles. Nex, Angel of Death (a separate encounter to Nex) is also found here which is the sole location of unlocking the best damage boosting Curses in the game and also drops T92 Magic dual wield weapons. It’s also worth noting that the armor dropped by Nex is relevant for upgrading certain sets of armor like Tectonic to Elite Tectonic. The Vorago boss fight is also located in Asgarnia, offering players using the Magic combat style T90 weapons and Tectonic power armor. The Temple of Aminishi Elite Dungeon is also available with Asgarnia unlocked, which comes with Ancient Scales and Masuta’s Warspear, a T82 halberd type Melee weapon. For Ranged combat oriented players, the Queen Black Dragon can also provide the T80 Royal Crossbow.
