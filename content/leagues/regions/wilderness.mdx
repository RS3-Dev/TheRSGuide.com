The Wilderness in RuneScape covers a lot of mid game content including two Elite Dungeons, a massive variety of Slayer creatures, as well as access to Daemonheim which is the hub for the Dungeoneering skill and upgrades. The Mage Arena can also be found here providing strong early game Magic upgrades as well as an Agility Course, an Archaeology Dig Site, and access to the highest tier of Ore in the game. The Dungeoneering shop also has multiple skilling upgrades related to Herblore, Crafting, Prayer, Archaeology and Summoning.

It’s also worth noting the Area Task Sets in the Wilderness are a large benefit of unlocking the region as they’re relatively easy to do for the extremely high amount of XP they award.

## Notable Gear

The Dungeoneering shop offers a huge amount of rewards scaling from T55 up to T90 weaponry, most notably the T80 Chaotic weaponry. The shop also allows the player to purchase the Chaotic Grimoire, a slightly weaker but easier to obtain alternative to Erethdor’s Grimoire. The Dragonkin Laboratory and The Shadow Reef are two Elite Dungeons gained through access to the Wilderness that offer the player powerful Melee ability upgrades, Tectonic Magic armor, and the Eldritch Crossbow; a weapon with a powerful endgame Ranged special attack. Across all of the various Slayer monsters in the Wilderness there are also numerous useful items including Revenant gear like Statius’ Warhammer as well as Abyssal items like the Abyssal Wand, Scourge, and Mask.
