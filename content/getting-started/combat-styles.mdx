## Necromancy

Necromancy is RuneScape's newest combat style, designed specifically for newer players to be more intuitive, more tutorialized, and more powerful than the other styles in the early and mid game.

It involves both traditional abilities to deal damage as well as powerful undead conjures which deal damage, provide damage reduction, and provide healing.

Because of this, **Necromancy is recommended for newer players** as a means to ease into the combat system and fast-track to the late game.

## Magic

Magic is one of the 3 core styles. It offers a powerful blend of damage, utility, and convenience by taking advantage of the normal and ancient spellbooks. It relies on high-high damage basic abilities, critical strikes, and gains significant power in the endgame with legendary weapons.

## Melee

Melee is one of the 3 core styles. It is one of the most perilous, but rewarding combat styles to use, and is one of the most enjoyable and popular styles in RuneScape. It relies on high-hitting abilities, a wide array of special attacks, and situational use of both dual wield and 2h weapons.

## Ranged

Ranged is one of the 3 core styles. It can offer solid damage, but lacks utility and convenience compared to the other styles especially at the endgame. It's seen as a high skill ceiling combat style that requires intimate knowledge of the combat system to master - but can offer very high damage when executed correctly.
